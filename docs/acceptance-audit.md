The first usable release's six technical steps are represented locally: register,
reuse settings, preserve searchable evidence, produce a cited briefing, correct an
interpretation, and preserve previous valid state on failure. This is still short
of the complete requested roadmap because model evaluation, critical-context quality,
owner resumption measurement and remaining release/activation gates are open.

Next work should address these gaps rather than add unrelated connectors or expand
capture scope. The user's deferral of hosting access remains in effect. No completion,
model quality, native acceptance or human benefit is asserted by this audit.
